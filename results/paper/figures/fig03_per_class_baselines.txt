Per-class IoU on the 997 test frames of the 4 official test sequences under the official rule (a class is scored only on frames where the ground truth contains it), for the proposed model and the baselines trained here under one recipe (Ours (CAFormer-S18), SegFormer-B0, U-Net (scratch)). A class with no ground truth in the test set is not drawn. Thin and rare classes (thread, needle) separate the models least reliably; one training run per model, so differences of a few points are within run-to-run variation. Not shown (it did not train stably in five attempts, one of them in full precision; see results/taskP/baselines.json): DeepLabV3+ (ResNet-18). Source: results/taskP/baselines.json.
