Accuracy against cost for every correction policy on the official test set: mean change in instrument IoU on the frames the gate flags (y) against mean latency per frame (x, log scale). Latency is composed from components measured on this machine (RTX 3050 Ti laptop GPU, CPU for the correction steps): the selector and the every-frame fusion rows are the measured M6, M4, M5 latencies of Task 6; routed policies add the measured per-action time on the flagged share of frames; the oracle runs every candidate. Instrument IoU counts a class if it is in the ground truth or the prediction, so most of the A3 gain is removal of invented classes (Task B). Source: results/taskB/policy_table.json, results/task6/final_results.json, work_v2/actions.
